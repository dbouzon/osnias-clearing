Osnias Clearing

Osnias Clearing is a multichain EVM clearing infrastructure designed to coordinate collateral-backed positions across independent lending and escrow protocols. It uses a 91-day clearing cycle to let positions circulate and progressively net before final settlement, while keeping clearing strictly separated from custody, lending and governance.

01 · Problem

Blockchain optimized transaction speed, not clearing time

Collateral and liquidity are fragmented across chains and protocols, while settlement is commonly immediate and gross. Osnias Clearing introduces a shared time-based clearing layer so reciprocal positions have time to circulate and offset before the remaining net balance is finally settled.

02 · Solution

A common clearing layer across EVM ecosystems

91-Day Clearing Cycle

Positions circulate during a defined operating cycle, allowing progressive compensation before final reconciliation.

Multichain EVM Integration

Participating EVM networks connect through integration layers while local lending and collateral custody remain with independent protocols.

Strict Functional Separation

Clearing, custody, lending and governance are separate domains with distinct technical roles and trust boundaries.

03 · Economic Principle

Horizontal clearing leverage

Financial leverage increases simultaneous exposure. Clearing leverage increases cumulative economic throughput over time.
Osnias seeks capital efficiency through circulation and multilateral netting rather than by stacking immediate debt on top of the same collateral.
04 · Architecture

Three distinct protocol domains

OSNIAS

Governance and strategic-reserve token. Canonical governance state on Sei, with separate economic mobility across compatible EVM environments.

ORUSD

Restricted USD-denominated clearing-register token. EOA-focused circulation with explicit mint/burn and oracle settlement boundaries.

OEURO

Restricted EUR-denominated clearing-register token following the same clearing-only architecture and separation principles.

05 · Current Stage

Publicly documented and entering ecosystem integration

Public
