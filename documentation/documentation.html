01 · Project Overview

Osnias Clearing technical scope

Osnias Clearing is a blockchain clearing infrastructure built around a canonical clearing layer on Sei Network, restricted clearing-register tokens (ORUSD / OEURO), a strict functional separation between clearing and settlement, and NNN nodes responsible for independent settlement execution across 1-, 4- and 13-week clearing cycles.

02 · Architecture Summary

Core architecture

Canonical Clearing on Sei

Sei hosts the clearing register, ISO 20022 financial messaging, the Sei Controller and the temporal oracle. Clearing cycles of 1, 4 and 13 weeks. Osnias calculates net positions but does not execute settlement.

EVM Escrow Infrastructure

Connected EVM environments host local Osnias-ID registries, collateral escrow arrangements and the infrastructure of registered NNN nodes. Technical controller messages between chains are encrypted and signed.

Strict Functional Separation

Clearing and settlement are distinct functions. NNN nodes execute inter-NNN and intra-NNN settlement independently. Osnias does not take custody of collateral and does not execute settlement.

03 · Clearing Principle

Horizontal clearing leverage

Clearing identifies accepted obligations, reconciles them and offsets them on a multilateral basis. Its output is a set of net positions and settlement instructions.
Osnias calculates the netting result but does not execute settlement. Settlement is executed exclusively by NNN nodes under their own legal and regulatory framework. Clearing and settlement are distinct functions governed by separate technical states and applicable legal frameworks.

04 · Architecture

Two networks, three clearing components

ORUSD

USD-denominated clearing-register token on Sei. Restricted to eligible EOA accounts registered in the Osnias network. Not designed for market circulation, bridging or smart-contract routing.

OEURO

EUR-denominated clearing-register token following the same canonical clearing architecture. ISO 4217 currency code embedded in the Osnias-ID at creation.

Osnias-ID

19-digit clearing account identifier (KK-NNN-MMM-WW-SSSSSSS-XX). Links the canonical Sei registry to the local EVM registry. MOD-97 checksum. uint64 on-chain storage.
