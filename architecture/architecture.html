Architecture

Osnias Clearing is designed as a blockchain clearing infrastructure with a canonical Sei EVM execution layer, restricted clearing-register tokens, oracle-controlled settlement boundaries and a strict separation between clearing, governance, custody and lending.

01 · Inter-chain Architecture

A hard boundary between clearing cycle on Sei chain and settlement on EVM chain

The diagram below shows the two-network architecture of Osnias Clearing. Sei Network is the canonical clearing chain. Connected EVM environments host local Osnias-ID registries, collateral escrow arrangements and the infrastructure of registered NNN nodes. Technical controller messages between the two networks are encrypted and signed; they carry no payment instructions.

Architectural invariant: Osnias calculates the netting result but does not execute settlement. NNN nodes execute settlement at two levels: inter-NNN settlement of residual obligations between nodes and intra-NNN settlement for accounts registered with the same node.
02 · Core Architecture

Canonical clearing layer on Sei

Sei hosts the canonical clearing register: clearing accounts, clearing-room parameters, register positions, cycle status and multilateral netting. The canonical layer incorporates structured financial messaging for the issuance, processing and acceptance of payment requests between participants.

Sei — Clearing register

Osnias-ID Registry

Global registry of clearing accounts on Sei. Each Osnias-ID identifies the connected chain, the NNN node, the ISO 4217 currency, the clearing cycle and the account sequence, with a MOD-97 checksum.

Sei — Financial messaging

ISO 20022 Messages

Structured financial messaging for the issuance, processing and acceptance of payment requests between participants. Three stages: Payment Request, Accept/Reject, Transfer recorded in the canonical clearing register.

Sei — Cycle control

Sei Controller & Oracle

The Sei Controller manages the clearing cycle, records accepted transfers and calculates net positions. The temporal oracle determines the applicable phase — Register, Clearing or Settlement — across 1-, 4- and 13-week cycles.

03 · Execution Layer

Commitment register on the canonical clearing layer

Within the clearing layer on Sei, participants exchange commitment orders — not payments. The canonical register records accepted obligations, tracks their status across the clearing cycle and calculates net positions at cycle close. At no point does Osnias Clearing access, move or authorise movement of custody assets held in client escrow accounts. Settlement of net positions is executed exclusively by the operator of each NNN node, under their own responsibility, both internally within the node and between nodes across the network.

04 · Cross-Architecture Identifier

Osnias-ID — the common reference across the architecture

Osnias-ID is the transversal clearing-account identifier across the entire Osnias architecture. Its 19-digit format identifies the connected chain, the NNN node responsible for the account, the ISO 4217 currency, the clearing cycle and the account sequence, with a MOD-97 checksum. It links the canonical Sei clearing register to the corresponding local EVM registry, without treating a blockchain address as the corporate identity.

Sei — clearing side

Canonical registry

The global Osnias-ID registry on Sei associates each clearing account with its NNN node, currency and cycle. Novation events are recorded as on-chain state transitions linked to the relevant Osnias-IDs.

EVM — escrow side

Local EVM registry

Each connected EVM network holds a local Osnias-ID registry matching the native escrow account with its Osnias-ID. One Osnias-ID per currency and cycle per participant.

Format

KK-NNN-MMM-WW-SSSSSSS-XX

19 decimal digits — uint64 / 8-byte on-chain storage. Currency (ISO 4217) and clearing cycle (weeks) are fixed at creation. MOD-97 checksum. Fail closed on any mismatch.

05 · Functional Separation

Clearing is not custody, lending or DeFi

The clearing-register contracts do not implement customer deposits, custody, lending, collateral valuation, liquidity pools, farming or native conversion between OSNIAS and ORUSD/OEURO. External custody or escrow arrangements remain outside the clearing-token contracts.

Clearing — Sei

Clearing function

Identifies accepted obligations, reconciles them under clearing-room rules and offsets them on a multilateral basis. Produces net positions and settlement instructions. Does not itself discharge the payment obligation.

Settlement — NNN nodes

Settlement function

Settlement discharges the residual obligation through transfer of the relevant settlement asset. Osnias does not perform that transfer. NNN nodes execute inter-NNN and intra-NNN settlement under their own legal and regulatory framework.

Regulatory perimeter

No DEX, AMM or CEX

Osnias does not operate a DEX, AMM or CEX and does not provide leveraged trading or investment services. ORUSD and OEURO are not designed for market circulation, bridging or smart-contract routing.

06 · Clearing Time Architecture

Operating Cycle and Controls

Each clearing room defines its currency, participants and cycle duration. The temporal oracle on Sei determines the applicable phase and applies the same phase status to the clearing register. Osnias Clearing operates 1-, 4- and 13-week clearing cycles. At the cycle cut-off, accepted positions are time-stamped and frozen for calculation before residual settlement instructions are released to the responsible NNN nodes.
