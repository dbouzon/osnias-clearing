Osnias Oracles

Transparent clearing records. Protected security infrastructure.

Osnias Clearing uses a distributed Osnias Oracle architecture to connect the canonical clearing environment on Sei with approved lender and escrow infrastructures across participating EVM blockchains.

The architecture is designed around strict separation of powers. Osnias Oracles verify and exchange authenticated information, while lenders and escrow protocols retain exclusive authority over their own smart contracts and collateral.

02 · Osnias Oracle Architecture

Two environments, one strict separation model

Osnias Clearing distinguishes between Osnias Oracles operating inside the protected Sei security domain and Osnias Oracles operating on external EVM blockchains.

Sei

Osnias Oracles on Sei

Osnias Oracles on Sei support the canonical clearing environment. Their responsibilities include mint / burn processing, clearing-register integrity verification and the production of periodic registry snapshots used by the Osnias clearing cycle.

Participating EVM blockchains

Blockchain-side Osnias Oracles

A blockchain-side Osnias Oracle operates as a passive observation and communication component. It listens to approved smart contracts, verifies relevant state and exchanges authenticated requests with the Osnias clearing environment.

Separation principle. Osnias Oracles may observe, verify and exchange requests. They do not acquire administrative authority over independent lender or escrow smart contracts.
03 · Osnias Oracles on Sei

Protected canonical clearing infrastructure

The Osnias Oracles on Sei belong to the private security domain of Osnias Clearing. They support the integrity and continuity of the canonical clearing register.

Mint / Burn

Clearing-token supply processing

Dedicated Osnias Oracles on Sei support the controlled mint and burn processes associated with the canonical clearing register.

Register integrity

Continuous verification

Osnias Oracles verify consistency of the canonical register and help detect conditions that could affect clearing integrity.

Clearing snapshots

Periodic registry state

Osnias Oracles support the production and verification of registry snapshots required by the clearing cycle.

Private security domain. The internal architecture, operational configuration and sensitive implementation details of the Osnias Oracles on Sei are not intended for public disclosure.
04 · Blockchain-Side Osnias Oracles

Open, passive and adapted to each partner environment

Osnias Oracles operating on external EVM blockchains follow a passive model. Their purpose is to observe and verify relevant smart-contract state and communicate with approved lender / escrow infrastructures.

Observe

Read approved smart contracts

A blockchain-side Osnias Oracle may inspect approved lender or escrow smart-contract state required for clearing verification.

Verify

Validate relevant conditions

The Osnias Oracle verifies that the observed state is coherent with the authenticated request being exchanged.

Communicate

Exchange authenticated requests

The Osnias Oracle communicates verified requests and state information without obtaining execution authority over the partner protocol.

Passive-by-design invariant. A blockchain-side Osnias Oracle does not move collateral, release escrow, originate credit, liquidate positions or execute financial operations on behalf of a lender or escrow provider.
05 · Partner-Specific Implementation

Designed with each lender / escrow infrastructure

Osnias does not impose a single generic external oracle connector on every protocol. Each blockchain-side Osnias Oracle implementation will be discussed with the relevant lender / escrow partner and adapted to the technical and security requirements of the environment concerned.

Blockchain security

Network-specific rules

The Osnias Oracle implementation must respect the security characteristics, operational practices and technical constraints of the EVM blockchain on which it operates.

Lender security

Partner-specific controls

Where necessary, the Osnias Oracle interface is adapted to the security requirements, contract architecture and governance model of the lender concerned.

Escrow security

No transfer of authority

Integration does not transfer custody or administrative authority to Osnias. The escrow provider remains responsible for its own contracts and assets.

Partner integration principle. Blockchain-side Osnias Oracle interfaces are partner-specific and will be designed in consultation with each lender / escrow infrastructure.
06 · Bidirectional Separation of Powers

Neither domain controls the other

The Osnias clearing domain and the lender / escrow domain are intentionally separated. They can exchange information and authenticated requests without acquiring privileged authority over one another.

Osnias domain

No authority over collateral

  • No administrative control over lender smart contracts.
  • No authority to release escrowed assets.
  • No authority to transfer collateral.
  • No authority to originate lender credit.
Lender / Escrow domain

No authority over Osnias clearing supply

  • No control over Osnias Oracles on Sei.
  • No direct authority over mint / burn processing.
  • No administrative control over the canonical clearing register.
  • No unilateral authority over clearing-token supply.
Bidirectional control-separation invariant. Osnias controls its clearing domain. Independent lenders and escrows control their collateral domain. Neither controls the other.
07 · End-User Control

Critical release actions remain voluntary

The architecture preserves end-user control over the actions that initiate or finalize the release of collateral. Osnias does not substitute itself for the user on the lender / escrow interface.

Osnias side

Voluntary user action

The end user initiates the relevant clearing-side action voluntarily through the Osnias environment.

Lender / Escrow side

Independent voluntary action

The end user separately interacts with the lender / escrow environment to initiate and finalize collateral release according to the partner protocol's own rules.

Dual-user-action principle. Osnias cannot release collateral on behalf of the user, and a lender cannot substitute itself for the user inside the Osnias clearing domain.
08 · Security Model

Private security on Sei · passive integration externally

Osnias applies different disclosure and implementation policies to the canonical Sei security domain and to external blockchain-side Osnias Oracles.

Sei security

Private security domain

Sensitive implementation details of the Osnias Oracles on Sei remain protected and are not part of the public technical disclosure layer.

External EVM security

Partner-adapted implementation

Blockchain-side Osnias Oracles are implemented according to the security model of the relevant blockchain and, where appropriate, the specific requirements of each lender / escrow partner.

Verification

Continuous separation controls

Static analysis, invariant testing and deployment monitoring are intended to verify that no unauthorized control path appears between the clearing domain and external lender / escrow infrastructure.

09 · Transparency & Confidentiality

Transparent registers. Protected oracle security.

Osnias Clearing aims for maximum transparency where transparency improves auditability: public register state, published contract rules, verifiable clearing records and observable protocol outcomes.

Public

What should be auditable

  • Clearing-register state.
  • Published smart-contract rules.
  • Verifiable clearing outcomes.
  • Public technical documentation.
  • Published production contract references.
Protected

What remains private

  • Internal Osnias Oracle security architecture on Sei.
  • Sensitive operational configuration.
  • Private security procedures.
  • Protected infrastructure details.
  • Information whose disclosure would increase attack surface.
Regulatory access. Protected security information may be made available to a competent regulatory authority where appropriate, subject to suitable confidentiality, controlled-access and reciprocal security safeguards.
10 · Security Philosophy

Our security is our contract.

Public trust in Osnias Clearing is intended to rest on verifiable contracts, transparent registers, explicit rules and auditable outcomes — not on publication of sensitive security infrastructure.

Our security is our contract. Osnias Clearing publishes what must be auditable and protects what must remain secure.
