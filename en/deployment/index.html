Components and evidence

01

EVM core, identity and governance

The Solidity/EVM core is designed to remain portable across compatible networks. The 1W, 9W and 13W temporal oracles, Osnias-ID and V2 node registries, node-scoped counters and authorisation rules are implemented and tested. Bilateral governance controls chamber creation and administration by currency and cycle.

✓M38-4: 252 out of 252 Foundry tests passed; Anvil integration scenarios documented.

The operational trade-obligation registry and finality controls remain to be integrated.

02

Node Manager and Protocol Manager

Two TypeScript applications provide node and protocol management interfaces. They include wallet-based authentication, access controls, chamber views and locally tested governance procedures.

✓Technical demonstrators with unit and local EVM integration tests.

ERP workflows, multi-node operations and real-world access control require qualification.

03

ISO 20022 banking messages

The Go module contains a catalogue of 30 ISO 20022 schema versions, XML reading and validation tools, test fixtures and data structures for a banking gateway independent of the EVM core.

✓Schema library and software components available in the repository.

M14 qualification retains two blocking findings relating to proof of effective XSD validation and timeout handling in certain validators.

04

EBICS H005: ten development stages

M39-T01 to T10 cover the EBICS H005 schemas, INI/HIA envelopes, key handling, X002 XMLDSig authentication, two-phase FUL/FDL transfers and HPB corrections. Captures were checked against XSDs and signatures independently verified with xmlsec1.

✓M39-T10: 49 Go EBICS and 20 XMLDSig tests passed, according to test reports.

Key-management responses, S002/A006 order signatures and interoperability with an independent bank remain to be qualified.

05

Escrow and bank oracle

The architecture envisages dedicated escrow accounts per Osnias-ID, currency and cycle. Go modules and simulators prepare the monitoring of balances, locks and movements. The bank oracle is independent of the EVM network.

✓Attestation structures, processing modules and local simulations.

No real-bank connection or real escrow-account attestation has yet been demonstrated.

06

Next: a complete pilot cycle

The next work must connect admission of B2B trade obligations, the off-chain Go multilateral netting engine, publication of net positions to EVM, ERP APIs and bank settlement messaging. Results must be reproducible and traceable.

✓Target: an integrated demonstrator with end-to-end tests.

No production clearing or bank settlement service is claimed.

Qualification scope

These indicators come from reports in the private development repository. They do not constitute an independent security audit, banking certification or regulatory approval. The documented work is conducted in development or test environments.
