Osnias-ID

Osnias-ID is an internal clearing account identifier within the Osnias Clearing network. Its 19-digit format binds the account to a currency, a clearing cycle and a participant registry, independently of a wallet’s technical address. It is neither a legal identity nor a regulatory authorization.
