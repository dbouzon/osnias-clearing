Client Onboarding Through a Node

Book VI describes the operational process through which a company becomes a participant of an Osnias Clearing node, funds an escrow contract with native EURC on Ethereum and gains access to the canonical clearing layer on Sei. It turns the identity, architecture, clearing, synchronization and security principles established in the preceding Books into a coherent admission, activation and exit process.

Scope

Admission, activation and exit

The workflow covers node admission, onboarding controls, escrow funding, activation of clearing access, and the conditions under which a participant exits the system.

Institutional onboarding for digital clearing infrastructure

Osnias Clearing onboarding is designed as a controlled participant-admission process for companies joining the network through a node. The workflow links identity, node membership, Osnias-ID assignment, escrow funding and activation of clearing access into one operational sequence. Rather than treating onboarding as a simple wallet connection, Book VI frames it as the entry point into an institutional digital-asset clearing infrastructure with explicit responsibilities for the participant and the node operator.

The funding step uses native EURC on Ethereum deposited into an escrow architecture external to the canonical clearing register on Sei. This supports a non-custodial onboarding model in which Osnias does not directly hold participant funds, while the node manages the operational path between admission, escrow funding and clearing activation. The separation between escrow, identity and clearing access is intended to make the participant lifecycle auditable and to preserve the distinction between custody, clearing and settlement functions.

Book VI covers the full participant lifecycle from admission to controlled exit. It therefore includes onboarding controls, identity activation, escrow funding, access to the Sei clearing layer, renewal or update of participant credentials and off-boarding conditions. The documentation is intended for node operators, financial institutions, infrastructure partners, regulators and auditors assessing how corporate participants enter, operate within and leave the Osnias Clearing network.
