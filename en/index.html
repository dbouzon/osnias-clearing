Multilateral netting for corporate treasury

Net recurring B2B obligations. Streamline settlement.

Version 2.0 · 16 October 2026 · Updated: 17 October 2026

Fewer gross settlement flows · Clearer net positions · Better liquidity planning

Osnias Clearing is developing a B2B clearing network that brings recurring commercial obligations into a common netting process. Eligible reciprocal obligations are aggregated within local nodes and across the network to calculate the residual net positions to be settled.

For corporate treasury teams, the aim is to reduce gross settlement flows and plan the liquidity needed at each cycle’s close. The potential benefit depends on the pattern of reciprocal obligations, participation in the network and the applicable escrow requirements.

The model separates the clearing ledger from settlement assets held at node level. Companies and node operators can explore the rules, review the process and assess the operational and liquidity effects through B2B pilots.

01 · From obligations to net positions

A defined process before settlement

Record eligible obligations, aggregate reciprocal flows and calculate residual net positions. The proposed process gives participants a common framework for preparing settlement at the end of each cycle.

The model provides for local nodes, each serving participating companies within its own operational and legal environment. Netting is designed to take place within each node, followed by inter-node clearing of residual positions.

A pilot is particularly relevant to a group of companies connected by recurring purchases and sales. Reviewing their flows helps establish whether reciprocal obligations offer sufficient netting potential and whether the proposed calendar fits their treasury operations.

The target architecture combines a common ledger on Sei with separately escrowed settlement assets on Ethereum at node level. Published deployment references identify the technical components available for review; the full network process remains to be evaluated through pilots.

The 1-week, 9-week and 13-week cycles cover technical pilots, B2B commercial obligations over a 63-day cycle, and eligible non-commercial obligations respectively.

02 · Operational and liquidity efficiency

Reduce gross flows. Plan for the net amount.

Multilateral netting can reduce the volume of funds that needs to move at settlement. Pilots are intended to assess the effect on treasury operations and liquidity requirements using the obligations and conditions of each participating network.

Settlement flows

Settle residual net positions

Eligible reciprocal obligations are netted before settlement. The resulting residual positions replace the corresponding gross bilateral settlement amounts within the clearing process.

Liquidity planning

Understand the funding requirement

Treasury teams can assess the net amount due alongside escrow requirements. Liquidity savings depend on the obligations that can be offset and the conditions of the pilot.

Operational visibility

Prepare for cycle close

A shared cycle calendar and recorded positions support the preparation of settlement. Participants can review their obligations and monitor how their net position develops.

03 · Clearing and settlement roles

Shared netting logic, segregated settlement assets

In the target architecture, the common ledger records obligations and net positions, while settlement assets are segregated at node level. Participants, node managers and the Osnias manager have distinct permissions.

SEI

Common clearing ledger

Sei is intended to host the canonical ledger of obligations, participant positions, cycles and the states required for netting calculations.

ETHEREUM

Node-level escrow and settlement

The model provides for assets escrowed on Ethereum, with participants retaining their private keys. Escrow adjustments follow the cycle windows; authorised withdrawals go to each participant’s dedicated withdrawal address.

OSNIAS

Clearing separated from settlement

Osnias develops the clearing logic. The node manager performs the prescribed netting operations for its own node; the Osnias manager configures nodes and their escrow limits. These roles do not confer a general right to dispose of participant assets.

04 · A common settlement calendar

Defined cycles for recurring obligations

The 9-week B2B cycle spans 63 days between settlements. A 13-week cycle covers eligible non-commercial obligations, while the 1-week cycle supports technical environments and pilots.

Principle. Reciprocal obligations are aggregated during the cycle, netted locally and then across nodes, and only residual net positions are settled at maturity.
