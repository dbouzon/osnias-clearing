B2B multilateral clearing infrastructure

Multi-node B2B clearing network

Version 2.0 · 16 October 2026

Multilateral netting · Net settlement · Liquidity & working capital

Osnias Clearing enables companies with recurring B2B obligations to net reciprocal positions before settlement. Flows are aggregated within local nodes and then across the network so that only residual net positions need to be settled.

The network is designed for mid-market companies and larger enterprises, value chains and business ecosystems in which customers, suppliers and partners generate recurring obligations. Multilateral netting reduces the number and volume of gross settlements and limits the liquidity required for final settlement.

The 9-week cycle covers B2B commercial obligations. Aggregating reciprocal obligations before settlement supports the management of cash flows, settlement liquidity and working capital. The 13-week cycle covers eligible non-commercial obligations, while the 1-week cycle is used for technical environments and pilots.

01 · B2B clearing

Multilateral netting of recurring obligations between companies

Osnias Clearing converts a set of bilateral gross settlements into net positions to be settled. Recurring economic obligations are recorded, aggregated and netted during a cycle in order to reduce the cash movements required at final settlement.

The network is structured around local nodes. Each node serves its corporate clients within its own operational and legal environment. Reciprocal obligations are netted within the node; residual positions then participate in inter-node clearing.

The model targets companies that trade repeatedly within one economic area, across several areas or inside the same value chain. Recurring customer-supplier flows create opportunities for multilateral netting and liquidity savings.

The common clearing ledger operates on Sei. Assets intended for settlement remain segregated at node level on Ethereum. This architecture keeps net-position calculation functionally separate from the settlement of assets.

The 1-week, 9-week and 13-week cycles cover technical pilots, B2B commercial obligations over a 60-day horizon, and eligible non-commercial obligations respectively.

02 · Value for companies

Reduce gross settlements, reduce settlement liquidity, manage the net position

The economic value of clearing comes from netting reciprocal obligations before settlement. The same volume of economic activity can therefore require fewer cash movements at final settlement.

Liquidity

Working capital

Manage treasury and working capital

Clearing cycles make the amount due at maturity visible and structure the management of recurring obligations, treasury and working capital.

03 · Functional architecture

A common clearing ledger, segregated settlement assets

Obligation processing, net-position calculation and clearing cycles remain separate from the holding and settlement of assets.

SEI

Common clearing ledger

Sei hosts the canonical ledger of obligations, participant positions, cycles and the states required for netting calculations.

ETHEREUM

Node-level escrow and settlement

Assets intended for settlement are escrowed separately on Ethereum under the responsibility of node operators.

OSNIAS

Clearing separated from settlement

Osnias operates the clearing logic and infrastructure. Custody, escrow and settlement of assets remain at node level.

04 · Economic cycles

1 week · 9 weeks B2B · 13 weeks

The 1W cycle is used for technical environments and pilots. The 9W cycle organises B2B commercial obligations over a 60-day horizon. The 13W cycle covers eligible non-commercial obligations.

Principle. Reciprocal obligations are aggregated during the cycle, netted locally and then across nodes, and only residual net positions are settled at maturity.
