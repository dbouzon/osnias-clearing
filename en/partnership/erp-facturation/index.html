A straightforward commission rate decreasing linearly with notional volume

Proposed commission rate: 0.30% up to €150,000, decreasing linearly between €150,000 and €600,000, then 0.15% from €600,000. The rate is calculated separately on each customer's notional volume, never on the node's aggregate volume. One rate applies to each customer, without cumulative tiers.

The proposed revenue-sharing model allocates 75% of commissions to the node operator and 25% to Osnias. Billing definitions and final terms remain contractual.

The node operator neither holds nor controls customer funds. Funds remain at banks in segregated escrow accounts. This separation does not in itself remove potential regulatory obligations.

Illustrative example: on €300,000 of notional volume, the rate is 0.25%, generating €750 of total commission, of which €562.50 goes to the node and €187.50 to Osnias.
