A progressive partnership, from design to market

Phase 0 · Existing foundation

Books I–VII, initial testnet components, simulations and regulatory scoping provide a documented starting point.

Phase 1 · Clean code: recommend and contribute

From the outset, partners advise on robustness, modular architecture, ERP interoperability, API specifications, and software quality. Osnias Core remains responsible for the shared protocol.

Phase 1 · Milestone-based POC under SEP

A French société en participation (SEP), a contractual joint venture, structures the POC. Each milestone has deliverables and acceptance criteria; partners authorize progress payments under agreed governance. A shared demonstrator enables initial multi-ERP tests.

Phase 2 · Operating company and assurance

Following POC acceptance, an operating company is expected to be incorporated, with founding partners entering its share capital under agreed terms. This phase seeks grants and funding and covers industrialization, jurisdictional work, advanced testing and independent security audits.

Phase 3 · Integration and commercialization

Subject to required technical and regulatory validations, ERP software vendors may integrate the service and appropriately qualified providers may consider operating nodes.

ERP and accounting: an alternative to invoice-by-invoice settlement

Osnias Clearing aims to net eligible B2B trade obligations through multilateral clearing cycles, with only residual net balances settled by their due dates. ERP and accounting systems retain invoice traceability and reconciliation.

ERP and accounting software

Connectors and APIs to handle obligations, clearing cycles, net positions and accounting reconciliation.

B2B electronic invoicing

A complementary capability alongside e-invoicing platforms and structured data exchanges.

Deferred settlement through clearing

An alternative settlement workflow at agreed due dates, without Osnias lending or automatically extending legal payment periods.

Clearing is separate from final settlement. Osnias does not extend credit. Integration and operation remain subject to applicable regulatory qualifications and requirements. No authorization or commercial outcome is presumed.

Prepare for ViDA 2030 before commercial launch

EU developments in digital reporting and electronic invoicing encourage software vendors to strengthen interoperability. Partners can plan clearing and reconciliation APIs from Phase 1. ViDA is neither an authorization nor a certification of Osnias.

A shared core, several partner roles

Osnias Core maintains the protocol’s integrity, releases and common components. Software vendors and integrators develop connectors and client journeys; node operators assume their own compliance and operational responsibilities.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to be an ERP software vendor to join?

No. Industrial, technology and financial partners are also welcome; ERP and accounting software vendors are especially relevant for integration.

How do partners influence the Clean Code refactoring phase?

They recommend architecture, API interfaces, test requirements and business scenarios from the outset of Phase 1.

Who controls POC milestone payments?

The Phase 1 SEP defines deliverables and acceptance criteria. Partners review them and authorize progress payments under agreed governance.

What happens after the POC?

An operating company is envisaged at the start of Phase 2, with founding partners becoming shareholders under agreed terms.

Is Osnias already certified for ViDA 2030?

No. ViDA is a relevant context for e-invoicing; Osnias’s regulatory classification and applicable authorizations still require confirmation.
