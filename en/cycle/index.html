1W, 9W and 13W Clearing Cycles

Osnias Clearing organizes multilateral clearing around three synchronized profiles: 1W testnet, 9W B2B commercial and 13W non-commercial. Each profile follows a common UTC reference calendar so that participants, nodes and clearing rooms operate from the same temporal framework. The objective is to make clearing obligations predictable, auditable and reproducible across jurisdictions and technical environments.

The cycle mechanism separates clearing from settlement. Transactions are aggregated during the active period and converted at the clearing date into net positions and net settlement obligations. This multilateral netting model is designed to reduce gross settlement flows, improve liquidity efficiency and preserve a clear distinction between the calculation of obligations and the subsequent monetary settlement performed by the relevant settlement infrastructure or node operator.

Cycle classification

1W — TESTNET: a technical validation profile reproducing the main state transitions within one UTC week. Its test calendar must be specified separately; the day-by-day adjustment window of long cycles is not assumed to apply.

9W — B2B commercial: cycle for commercial obligations between undertakings. The invoice may state “Payment method: CLEAR 9W”. The protocol envelope is nine weeks, with a 59-day operational window before settlement.

13W — non-commercial: cycle for eligible non-commercial obligations. The settlement-to-settlement envelope is 13 weeks (91 days), with an 87-day operational window.

UTC Schedule, Multilateral Netting and Net Settlement

Book 4 is the reference document for the timing model. The published v2.2 PDFs and workbook document the cycle profiles and calculation methodology. For long cycles, Monday, day 1, settles the previous cycle’s net positions. From Tuesday to Thursday, days 2 to 4, participants may add funds or withdraw the releasable amount to their registered dedicated withdrawal address, which is separate from the escrow address. Escrow is locked at the close of Thursday’s adjustment window. The active phase follows, then positions are frozen at cycle end for final netting. The exact Thursday UTC closing time remains to be specified. The 1W test calendar must be defined separately. Published files remain unchanged and should be read alongside current security and calendar clarifications.
