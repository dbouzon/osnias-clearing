Clearing cycles

Three cycle profiles share one weekly UTC calendar: 1W, 4W and 13W. Each clearing closes on a Monday, followed by a defined BURN window. Book 4 and its calculation workbook document the schedule.

Osnias Clearing organizes multilateral clearing around three synchronized cycle profiles: 1 week, 4 weeks and 13 weeks. Each profile follows a common UTC reference calendar so that participants, nodes and clearing rooms operate from the same temporal framework. The objective is to make clearing obligations predictable, auditable and reproducible across jurisdictions and technical environments.

The cycle mechanism separates clearing from settlement. Transactions are aggregated during the active period and converted at the clearing date into net positions and net settlement obligations. This multilateral netting model is designed to reduce gross settlement flows, improve liquidity efficiency and preserve a clear distinction between the calculation of obligations and the subsequent monetary settlement performed by the relevant settlement infrastructure or node operator.

Book 4 is the reference document for the timing model. It describes the 1W, 4W and 13W profiles, the weekly UTC calendar, the defined BURN windows and the calculation methodology associated with each cycle. The published PDF, Zenodo DOI and XLSX workbook provide a stable technical basis for implementation, review, audit and regulatory analysis of the Osnias Clearing cycle architecture.
