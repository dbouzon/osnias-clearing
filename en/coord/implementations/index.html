Implement COORD Specification 1.0

22 public vectors · any language · publishable results

COORD Specification 1.0 structures the collaboration of software agents (typically LLM-backed) under the authority of a human pilot. Conformance is proven by replaying 22 public vectors, with no account and no API key. Two independent reference engines (Go and Python) pass them today: be the third.

01 · What to replay

The 22 conformance vectors

The vectors are normative JSON scenarios in the coord-conformite-v1 format: 3 positive transitions, 9 negative transitions (explicit refusal motifs), 8 authorization configurations and 2 resume arbitrations.

In practice: fetch the coord/docs/conformite-v15/ folder (or the full archive), read the standard and the implementer guide (coord/docs/coord-guides.md), implement the closed state machine, then replay each vector: every attendu must match exactly, refusal motifs included.
02 · Publish your results

Transparency required, no recognition claimed

Publish your per-vector results (OK/KO), your environment (language, version, OS), the qualification of each test family (real / simulated / not executed) and your documented divergences.

Where: open an issue in the dbouzon/osnias-clearing repository with the [COORD-IMPL] prefix, or publish in your own repository and reference it. Never present your results as evidence of authentication security: adapters carry the trust boundary, the engine never authenticates (standard, section 11).
