1. Historical document and scope of its interpretation

The AMF Memorandum v2.0 is retained as a historical documentary reference. It presents the technical facts, assumptions and qualification questions formulated at publication. Its French and English PDFs and DOI remain available. This page does not modify those files or present them as an exhaustive description of the current architecture.

2. Current architecture and responsibilities

In the current design, the canonical register on Sei records obligations, positions and clearing states. These positions are not standalone transferable or tradable digital assets. Settlement assets remain in an external escrow and settlement layer. Node managers and their providers act according to the services actually performed and the applicable authorizations.

3. Qualification questions and dialogue with authorities

Scoping seeks to examine the functions of the operating company, node managers and settlement providers under applicable law. Technical separation of layers is not, by itself, sufficient to determine regulatory status. Discussions with authorities are preliminary; they do not constitute authorization, permission for production deployment or a definitive qualification.

Current Architecture and Regulatory Scoping

Osnias Clearing is presented as an infrastructure for calculating and clearing obligations, separate from custody and settlement functions. The clearing register on Sei records and reconciles positions; escrow and asset movements remain in a separate layer. Osnias does not hold users’ private keys or directly hold their funds.

Dialogue with AMF Innovation & Digital Finance concerns the functions described and their qualification under MiCA and the applicable French and European framework. Analysis must consider the assets used, technical permissions, mandates, contracts and responsibilities of each party, including any custody, transfers or payment services actually provided.

The project remains in development and on testnet; no definitive regulatory qualification is assumed. The historical memorandum and current documentation serve different purposes. The Architecture, Rules, Security and FAQ pages present the currently described operation and should be consulted alongside the historical document.

Multilateral Clearing and Services Actually Provided

The current architecture aims to record, aggregate and net obligations before settlement, then determine net balances. A position represented in a register must not be confused with the asset used to settle that balance. Position calculation, asset custody and execution of movements are therefore examined separately.

The node manager assumes the operational functions associated with its scope and authorizations. The operating company provides common rules, the register and technical protocol components. This allocation must be confirmed by contracts and actual permissions; it does not exempt any party from its own obligations.

The regulatory dossier must connect the services actually provided, the assets used and the responsible legal entity. Neither DLT use, the absence of direct custody by Osnias nor the term “node” alone determines the applicable regime. Material changes to functions or assets must be addressed in regulatory scoping discussions.
