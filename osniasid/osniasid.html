Osnias-ID

A Unique Participant Identifier for Multi-Chain Clearing. A compact, chain-independent 16-digit identifier designed to represent a clearing account across supported blockchain environments while keeping legal identity, clearing-account identity and technical wallet addresses as separate layers.

01 · Design Intent

Why Osnias-ID?

Osnias-ID was designed to provide Osnias Clearing with a compact reference that remains usable independently of the addressing system, account model or technical conventions of a specific blockchain.

Chain independent

One clearing-account reference

The identifier remains meaningful above the blockchain layer and does not depend on a chain-native wallet format.

Compact messaging

16 digits instead of repeated identity data

Clearing messages can transport the Osnias-ID and resolve detailed participant information through the registry where appropriate.

Counterparty resolution

Identify the clearing account

A receivable, invoice or clearing request can reference an Osnias-ID rather than relying solely on a hexadecimal wallet address.

02 · Identity Architecture

Legal Entity → Clearing Account → Technical Address

An Osnias-ID is not a company identifier. A company or institution may hold several Osnias-ID clearing accounts, all linked by the Osnias Registry to the same legal entity.

Core principle: An Osnias-ID is not a legal-entity identifier. It identifies a clearing account linked to an identified participant.
03 · Identifier Format

Deterministic 16-digit format

KK-NNN-AAAAAAAAA-XX
KK2 digits · Osnias-assigned blockchain/network code
NNN3 digits · Osnias-assigned participant/institution allocation
AAAAAAAAA9 digits · Registry-server unique participant value
XX2 digits · deterministic MOD-97 checksum

The complete identifier validates with I mod 97 = 1. The checksum detects common transcription errors but is not a cryptographic signature or proof of registry membership.

04 · Registry & Access Control

No Osnias-ID, no clearing request

The Osnias Registry is the authoritative resolution layer between a clearing account, its identified participant and its currently authorized operational addresses. Clearing admission is independent from checksum validity: a structurally valid identifier must also resolve to an eligible registry record.

Resolve

Wallet → Osnias-ID

The initiating or receiving technical address is resolved against an authorized clearing account.

Validate

Status and authorization

The system verifies registry status, wallet authorization and applicable network or transaction eligibility rules.

Fail closed

Admission invariant

If the required Osnias-ID cannot be resolved and validated, the clearing request is rejected.

05 · KK Network Registry

Pre-allocated and reserved network namespace

KK is a two-digit Osnias-assigned blockchain/network code. KK=00 is permanently reserved as a null/unassigned value; KK=01–99 provide 99 allocatable namespace positions. Public network codes are allocated only where the network can support the required programmable and auditable escrow architecture.

Allocated EVM

01–06

Ethereum, Sei EVM, Arbitrum One, Avalanche C-Chain, Base and BNB Smart Chain.

Allocated non-EVM

16–18

Solana, TRON and Stellar/Soroban, using their native programmable execution environments.

Reserved namespace

80–99

Institutional/central-bank reservations and Osnias internal protocol reservations. Reservation does not imply participation or endorsement.

07 · Scope Boundary

What Osnias-ID is — and is not

Osnias-ID is a project-specific clearing-account identifier and registry reference. It is not a bank account number, legal-entity identifier, wallet address, regulatory authorization number, security, token or credential of legal status. References to ISO 20022, LEI, UID/CHE, FINMA or other external frameworks are interoperability references only and do not imply certification or endorsement.
