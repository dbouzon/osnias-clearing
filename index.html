Worldwide Clearing Rail

The Jubilee Clearing Cycle

Osnias Clearing operates on four 91-day clearing cycles per year, based on a 4 × 13-week Jubilee structure. Final compensation takes place during the last three days of each cycle.

91 Days
Users have time to produce, exchange, circulate value and rebalance their positions before final settlement.
3-Day Compensation
The closing window is dedicated to reconciliation, validation and final compensation of the clearing cycle.
Velocity, Not Leverage
Economic strength comes from the internal velocity of exchange and reciprocal compensation within the cycle, not from financial leverage.
Core principle. Value is created by circulation, reciprocal exchange and compensation over time — not by the clearing token itself.

91-Day Clearing Cycle

Osnias Clearing separates the time of exchange from the time of final settlement. Transactions and clearing entries can circulate immediately inside the cycle, while the final net position is settled only after participants have had time to offset reciprocal commitments.

Continuous clearing
Day 1 → Day 88
Movements are recorded on the blockchain and positions evolve throughout the cycle. Participants retain time to trade, receive payments and naturally compensate reciprocal obligations.
Dated statement
Day 88
A dated clearing statement is produced near the end of the cycle. Participants have a short review period to contest a discrepancy before final execution.
Final settlement
Day 91
After the review window, the final net position is executed through the settlement architecture. The objective is to settle the residual balance, not every individual movement one by one.
Time is part of the clearing design. The 91-day period is not a technical delay: it is the economic window that allows reciprocal positions to circulate and compensate before final settlement. This operating cycle sits inside a broader architecture inspired by Jubilee cycles and periodic economic rebalancing.

Architecture

Osnias Clearing is a specialized clearing house. Its role is to record, reconcile and settle clearing positions. Fund custody, lending, credit and risk management remain with independent partner protocols.

OSNIAS Governance Layer
Functional separation
ORUSD USD Clearing Register
OEURO EUR Clearing Register
The functional scope of Osnias Clearing is limited to clearing. Exchange, liquidity, lending, custody and interoperability functions belong to other infrastructures or actors and are not the core purpose of the protocol.
A Multichain EVM Clearing Rail

Osnias Clearing aims to create a common clearing layer across multiple EVM ecosystems. Networks retain their autonomy, protocols and economic models; Osnias Clearing provides a common settlement logic across compatible domains.

  • Arbitrum — integration with lending partners and institutional liquidity infrastructure.
  • Ethereum — reference EVM layer and access to established lending infrastructure.
  • Cronos — EVM environment focused on digital assets and financial services.
  • BNB Chain — transaction capacity and broad EVM ecosystem.
  • Base — EVM environment with strong application-layer integration.
  • Polygon — scalable EVM infrastructure and decentralized-finance ecosystem.
  • Sei — governance and clearing domain in the target architecture.
Lending partners → Clearing rail
Interoperable EVM domains
Osnias Clearing does not replace lending protocols. It aggregates clearing positions backed by funds managed by independent DeFi protocols on their respective EVM networks. Custody, financing, collateral management and credit risk remain outside the clearing-house function.
  • Financing and collateral managed by lending partners.
  • Collateral composed of stablecoins.
  • No customer deposits or customer funds collected by Osnias Clearing.
  • No credit granted by Osnias Clearing.
  • No leverage mechanism in the clearing rail.
  • USD clearing through ORUSD.
  • EUR clearing through OEURO.
  • Separate governance through OSNIAS.
  • Architecture compatible with multiple EVM networks.
Multichain EVM Lending integration Stablecoin collateral Clearing settlement
Functional scope. Osnias Clearing is designed as clearing and settlement infrastructure on EVM networks. The protocol is not intended to receive bank deposits, maintain traditional bank accounts or grant bank credit. Collateral assets are stablecoins and remain within the technical and contractual perimeter of the relevant lending partners.
Clearing Registers — ORUSD & OEURO
ORUSD
USD Clearing Register
ORUSD is a programmable clearing register represented by a restricted-circulation ERC-20 token. It is designed for settlement between authorized accounts according to explicit and auditable rules.
  • 6 decimals
  • Direct EOA → EOA circulation
  • Transfers to contracts are not part of the ordinary operating model
  • Minimum gross transfer: 0.501 ORUSD
  • Transfer fee: 0.15%, deducted from the gross amount
  • Mint / burn under manager control
  • Oracle requests valid for 24 hours
  • Anti-replay protection for external proofs
USD clearing EOA settlement Oracle attestation
OEURO
EUR Clearing Register
OEURO is the EUR-denominated clearing domain. Its role is distinct from that of a market instrument: it organizes settlement according to the rules of the infrastructure.
  • Architecture dedicated to EUR clearing
  • Documented and auditable settlement rules
  • Supply managed through explicit functions
  • Functional separation from OSNIAS governance
  • No native dependency on a market-liquidity layer
