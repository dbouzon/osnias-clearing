OSNIAS CLEARING
Clearing Infrastructure · Sei EVM

Multichain Clearing Project

A common clearing register for EVM-based financial ecosystems.

Osnias Clearing is a blockchain-based clearing house operating on Sei EVM. It provides a common clearing registry for positions backed by funds managed by independent DeFi protocols across EVM networks.

Clearing is strictly separated from custody, lending and credit activities.

01 · Functional Layers

One infrastructure, clearly separated functions

Osnias Clearing separates governance, clearing, custody and lending into distinct technical domains. Each public page below focuses on one part of the architecture.

OSNIAS

Governance Layer

Governance, strategic alignment and structural decisions are separated from the clearing registers.

ORUSD / OEURO

Clearing Registers

Restricted USD- and EUR-denominated clearing registers designed for direct account-to-account settlement.

Independent Partners

Custody & Lending

Collateral custody, lending, credit decisions and risk management remain with independent protocols.

03 · Why Osnias Clearing?

Clearing designed around time, separation and auditability

Time

91-day clearing cycle

Economic positions are given time to circulate, offset and rebalance before final net settlement.

Explore the cycle →
Separation

No deposits · No credit · No leverage

Osnias focuses on clearing. Custody, lending and collateral management remain outside the clearing-house function.

Explore the perimeter →
Execution

Built on Sei EVM

Sei provides the intended high-performance EVM execution layer for an auditable, high-volume clearing register.

Why Sei? →
Core principle. Value is created by circulation, reciprocal exchange and compensation over time — not by the clearing token itself.
