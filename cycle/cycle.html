OSNIAS

The governance token of the OSNIAS ecosystem

OSNIAS is designed to carry governance rights, long-term economic alignment and the strategic reserve associated with the token. Its design prioritizes a fixed supply, simple ownership rights, canonical governance on Sei and omnichain economic mobility across compatible EVM networks.

03 · Utility

What is OSNIAS used for?

OSNIAS is primarily a governance token. Its function is to represent economic and decision-making power within OSNIAS governance, while remaining a transferable and technically composable EVM asset.

Governance

Governance rights

Eligible OSNIAS held on the canonical Sei chain can contribute to voting power according to the applicable governance rules.

Strategic alignment

Long-term alignment

The token aligns holders, governance and strategic management without turning every operational decision into an on-chain vote.

Strategic reserve

Strategic reserve

The technical specification provides for a strategic-reserve role, with major uses potentially subject to reserved governance matters.

04 · Governance

A single canonical governance domain on Sei

The rule is simple: OSNIAS economic mobility may be multichain, but governance power remains localized on Sei. This architecture is designed to preserve an observable and deterministic electorate without reconstructing votes across multiple networks.

OSNIAS on Sei

Economic value + governance eligibility, subject to the applicable voting and delegation rules.

Single canonical
governance state
OSNIAS outside Sei

Economic value only. Positions held on other networks do not generate OSNIAS voting power.

Governance Locality Invariant. A token must not be able to exercise voting power simultaneously on Sei and on another blockchain. Governance does not aggregate remote votes, external staking positions or custodied representations outside Sei.
05 · Technical Specification

Design du token

OSNIAS favors a relatively simple, readable and auditable contract. Native functions serve token ownership, governance and omnichain mobility; external financial uses remain the responsibility of third-party protocols.

Name OSNIAS
Nature ERC-20 governance and strategic-reserve token.
Genesis supply 100,000,000 OSNIAS.
Decimals 6 decimals.
Token creation No arbitrary post-genesis token creation should be possible beyond the technical mechanisms required by the OFT design.
Burn Voluntary self-burn of the holder’s own tokens; no confiscation or administrative burn of third-party balances.
Vote ERC20Votes with delegation and checkpoints; governance power recognized on the canonical Sei state.
Permit ERC-2612 Permit for compatible signed approvals.
Omnichain LayerZero V2 OFT for economic mobility across supported EVM networks.
Management Constrained operational management: hard invariants are non-negotiable; reserved matters require the applicable governance vote.
Mainnet architecture Privileged administration intended to be protected by multisig, cold/emergency keys and an external registry.
06 · Economic Mobility

OSNIAS can circulate outside its governance chain

The OFT design allows OSNIAS to be used as an economic asset on compatible EVM networks without duplicating governance power. This separation between economic mobility and governance authority is a core characteristic of the token.

Self-custody

Transfer & custody

OSNIAS can be transferred and held in wallets or custody solutions compatible with supported networks.

External composability

Third-party integrations

Third-party protocols may technically use OSNIAS for staking, farming, lending, escrow, liquidity, collateral or other compatible EVM use cases.

No protocol promise

No market guarantee

Technical compatibility does not constitute a promise of yield, liquidity, listing, market depth or price appreciation.

07 · Invariants

What the contract must not allow to be bypassed

Supply integrity

Protected supply

No management authority may arbitrarily create tokens beyond the intended model.

Holder rights

Holder ownership

No arbitrary seizure or burn of balances held by third parties.

Governance locality

Voting only on Sei

Governance power remains attached to the eligible canonical representation on Sei.

OFT conservation

Omnichain accounting

Cross-chain movements must preserve the intended global supply accounting.

Reserved matters

Management limits

Decisions classified as reserved matters must follow the applicable governance procedure.

Operational security

Mainnet multisig

The production model is intended to replace sensitive single-key authorities with a multisignature architecture and separate recovery mechanisms.

Design principle. Governance should constrain management without replacing day-to-day management: smart contracts protect non-negotiable invariants; governance decides reserved strategic matters; the Token Manager retains execution discretion within approved limits.
