Atomic safety rule.
ORUSD is first transferred into the Sei Burn Controller. Collateral release is then requested on EVM. If collateral is confirmed released, the locked ORUSD is burned. If the 15-minute window expires, Sei enters CLOSING; ORUSD is refunded only after EVM confirms that the collateral reservation was cancelled and not released.
